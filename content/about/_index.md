My name is **Matthew D. Hall**, and I'm a post-graduate research student in the department of Automatic Control and Systems Engineering at [The University of Sheffield](https://www.sheffield.ac.uk).
I am a member of the [Natural Robotics Lab](https://www.sheffield.ac.uk/naturalrobotics), led by Dr. Roderich Groß, and [Sheffield Robotics](https://www.sheffield.ac.uk/sheffieldrobotics), helmed by Prof. Tony Prescott.

My research is concerned with methods of reconfiguration for modular robotic systems. More specifically, I aim to design and deploy a novel method of self-reconfiguration, whereby modules actively remove themselves from a starting structure. This work is funded by the EPSRC.

I also host a regular slot on a [radio show](./radio) produced by my friend Prof. Murray and broadcast on [Gaga Radio](https://www.gagaradio.org). The show is called Clear A Space, and is a Jazz/Funk/Soul journey accompanying a lighthearted take on life and emotions. My section is Wait For The Moment and comprises a selection of some of my favourite Jazz/Funk/Soul tunes revolving around a central emotion/vibe.
